Since 2000, on Sturt Street

Owner-run by Tania and Mick Alsop from the first day.

El Artista opened in 2000 as a two-person salon at 44 Sturt Street. Tania and Mick Alsop wanted something that doesn't often come together: a genuinely professional salon with a fun, relaxed atmosphere, where clients and staff both enjoy their time.

It worked. By 2002 the salon had outgrown its first home and moved next door to number 42, into a larger space fully renovated and fitted out with designer furniture. It's since become recognised as one of the top full-service hair salons in Ballarat.

The team trains intensively, and keeps training, so every client gets today's techniques and trends alongside a classic sense of style.

“Hair design is a true art form. Your hair is your signature, and you must feel great about it every day.”
