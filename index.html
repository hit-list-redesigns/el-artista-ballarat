The house on Sturt Street

A two-person salon at 44 Sturt Street in 2000. Next door to number 42 by 2002.

Tania and Mick Alsop opened El Artista to be a professional salon with a fun, relaxed atmosphere, a place clients and staff both enjoy being. It soon outgrew its first home, moved next door, and grew into one of Ballarat's recognised full-service salons.

The team trains intensively and keeps training, so you get today's techniques with a classic sense of style.
